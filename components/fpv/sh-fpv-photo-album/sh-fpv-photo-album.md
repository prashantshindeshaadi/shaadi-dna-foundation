# sh-fpv-photo-album

**Purpose:** Her other photos, not already shown on the profile.

**Built from:** sh-fpv-section-title + sh-fpv-photo-album-stack + sh-button.

**Switches:** Gate = her album setting × the viewer (same rules as the photo area). Overflow = how many more photos ("+N" = N − 4).

**Rules:** counts only photos not already shown; drops when that count is 0.

**Taps:** View album → sh-fpv-album-view · lock CTAs as on the photo area.
