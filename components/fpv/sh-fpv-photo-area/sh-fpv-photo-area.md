# sh-fpv-photo-area

**Purpose:** The FPV photo slot: her photo (sh-photo-wrapper) with the gate overlay when it is locked, empty or requested.

**Built from:** sh-photo-wrapper + overlay (photo-locked icon, message, sh-button) or the no-photo illustration / requested portrait (sh-user-images) + sh-button.

**Switches:** Gate = why the photo is not shown (Visible, Accept gated, Upgrade gated, Tit for tat, No photo, Photo requested). Message = the line above the button.

**Rules:** icon (when used) 40 at the top, message box 302×48 (max 2 lines, centred), CTA sh-button Tertiary Medium; same position on every gate. Every locked photo is blurred strongly and says how to unlock it.

## Types

### Gate=Visible

**When:** the viewer may see her photos. Dots on if the album is visible and she has more than one photo.

### Gate=Accept gated

**When:** her photo is set to "Only members she likes" and she has not connected or accepted. Any plan.

### Gate=Upgrade gated

**When:** her photo is set to "Members she likes + Premium" and the viewer is Free.

### Gate=Tit for tat

**When:** Free viewer with no photo of his own, on a photo that is otherwise open.

### Gate=No photo

**When:** she has no photos. Same for every viewer; no tit for tat.

### Gate=Photo requested

**When:** she has no photos. Same for every viewer; no tit for tat.
