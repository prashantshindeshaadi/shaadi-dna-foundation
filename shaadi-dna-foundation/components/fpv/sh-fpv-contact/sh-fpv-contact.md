# sh-fpv-contact

**Purpose:** Her phone and email, always masked on the profile.

**Built from:** sh-fpv-section-title + sh-fpv-info-item (Masked Phone / Masked Email) + sh-fpv-locked-text (gold asterisks + Line lock) + sh-button.

**Switches:** Plan × State.

**Rules:** the number never shows unmasked here; only sh-fpv-sheet-phone-number shows it. One contact view is used on the first sheet open per profile. After the sheet closes this section returns to its usual state.

**Taps:** Unlock → premium plans · View Contact → phone-number sheet (Number shown, or No tokens when no views are left).

## Types

### Plan=Free, State=Gated

**When:** Free viewer. Gold mask + Unlock in the title.

### Plan=Premium, State=Unlock

**When:** Premium viewer. View Contact.

### Plan=Premium, State=Visible

Not used on the FPV (contact is never unmasked on the profile).

### Plan=Premium, State=Unverified

**When:** Premium viewer whose profile isn’t verified. Verify Now.

### Plan=Premium, State=Filtered

**When:** she limits who can see her contact. No View Contact.

### Plan=Free, State=Unlock

**When:** Free viewer on a Plus member’s profile: "{n} of 5 free views left" · View Contact.
