# sh-avatar-placeholder

**Purpose:** Muted grey silhouette wherever a member has no photo (no photo added, or photo requested). Gender × Aspect Ratio: 1:1 for circular avatars and mini cards, 4:5 for tall slots, Full for the full photo slot with an overlay.

**Editable:** Background (gradient on DNA neutral tokens) and Silhouette (a square holding sh-avatar-silhouette, centred, at sh-opacity/strong) are separate layers. Show silhouette off = background only (used behind sh-overlay, whose icon slot holds the silhouette).
