# sh-profile-card

**Purpose:** Member card for lists and feeds. Pick a Type, then edit Info, then the photo overlay.

**Built from:** sh-photo-wrapper (sh-user-images, top and bottom scrims, sh-overlay); sh-profile-card-info (name, age, badges, status, sh-info-detail facts, sh-tag-profile tags, sh-action-island); top row with sh-tag-profile and two translucent sh-icon-button.

**Switches:** Show top tags — the tag at top left (Promoted). Show photo actions — photo count and More at top right.

**Rules:** Type sets the photo state and the actions; never mix actions from two Types. One primary action per card. Text over the photo always sits on the info glass or a scrim. The last fact on each detail row has its separator off.

**Taps:** Photo count (View photos) opens her photos. More (More options) opens the member options. Every button and icon button keeps a 48 × 48 tap area.

## Types

### Type=Connect

**When:** a member you have not contacted yet.

**Purpose:** Default card with a connect prompt.

**Actions:** pre-written message with Edit; Connect (Primary, check).

### Type=Received

**When:** she sent you a connect request.

**Purpose:** She sent a connect — decline or accept.

**Actions:** Decline (Tertiary, close) and Accept (Primary, check). No note.

### Type=Post-Connect

**When:** you are already connected.

**Purpose:** Already connected — chat, WhatsApp, call.

**Actions:** verified badge on; note "To contact her directly" with Upgrade Now; Shaadi Chat, WhatsApp and Call as labelled icon buttons.

### Type=Gated

**When:** her photos are shared with Premium members only and you are not Premium.

**Purpose:** Photos locked — upgrade to view.

**Actions:** photo blurred under a lock overlay with Upgrade to View (Tertiary); Upgrade to Connect (Premium Solid). No note.

### Type=No photo

**When:** she has not added a photo.

**Purpose:** Member has no photo.

**Actions:** placeholder overlay "No Photo added yet" with Request a Photo (Tertiary); Connect Now (Primary, check). No note.

### Type=Photo requested

**When:** she has no photo and you have already requested one.

**Purpose:** The soft placeholder photo shows behind a scrim with a Photo Requested confirmation.

**Actions:** Photo Requested (check) on the overlay; Connect Now (Primary, check). No note.

### Type=Blocked

**When:** you blocked her.

**Purpose:** You blocked this member.

**Actions:** message "You blocked her"; Unblock (Tertiary). Photo count hidden. No note.

### Type=VIP

**When:** the profile is managed by a Shaadi VIP consultant.

**Purpose:** VIP-managed profile — contact goes through a Shaadi VIP consultant.

**Actions:** VIP badge beside the name; pre-written message with Edit; Call Consultant (Secondary, sub-label VIP Managed Profile) and Connect (Primary, check).

### Type=With Overlay

**When:** a member you have not contacted yet.

**Purpose:** Default card with a connect prompt.

**Actions:** pre-written message with Edit; Connect (Primary, check).

### Type=Declined

**Purpose:** she declined your Request to Connect: her photo and details stay, the action area shows that status and no action.
