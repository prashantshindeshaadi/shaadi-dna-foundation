# sh-navigation-bar

**Purpose:** The app's main navigation, built from six sh-navigation-item slots.

**Switches:** Device (Mobile bottom bar, Tablet bottom bar, Desktop side rail) · Show tab 6.

**Rules:** Every slot (Tab 1–6) is an exposed sh-navigation-item: pick its Tab, set Active on exactly one slot, and Show badge where a count matters. Default: Home, Matches, Inbox, Chat, Premium offer (phone) or Premium (tablet, desktop); Tab 6 = Updates, hidden. On phone keep five tabs: six do not fit 328. In screens the bar fills its container (floating: stretch with 16 margins).

**Taps:** each tab opens its section.
