# sh-navigation-item

**Purpose:** One tab of the app navigation. Tab picks what it is (Home, Matches, Inbox, Chat, Updates, Premium, Premium offer); Active shows the selected tab.

**Switches:** Tab · Layout (Stacked for phone, Inline for tablet and desktop) · Active · Show badge.

**Rules:** Icon and label come with the Tab, never typed by hand. Active = Filled icon in brand gradient + brand label; inactive = Line icon + tertiary label. Premium offer shows the 70% OFF ticket when inactive. Hugs its content with sh-space/8 sides, at least 48 wide.

**Taps:** the whole tab opens its section.
