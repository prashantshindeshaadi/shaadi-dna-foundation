# Hobby icons

**2026-10-21**

## What changed

- **45 hobby icons** were added to the Shaadi icon pack as `sh-icon/hobby-<hobby>`. They are Hugeicons Rounded, Line + Filled, sizes 24–12, with the stroke tied to the size tokens. Each icon is the one picked for its hobby in the hobby-icon experiment:
  - Movies, Binge-watching, Music, Biking, Travelling, Clubbing, Reading, Shopping, Sports, Theater & Events, Singing, Social media, Gaming, Running, Working out, Cycling, Trekking, Yoga & Meditation, Aerobics / Zumba, Walking, Swimming, Pets, Entrepreneurship, Foodie, Home decor, Vegan, Investments, News & Politics, Fashion & beauty, Social service
  - Writing, Acting, Cooking (chef hat), Poetry, Gardening, Photography, Blogging, Playing instruments, Content creation, Painting, Designing, DIY crafts, Doodling, Dancing, Anime (noodles)
- **The earlier hobby batch is replaced.** Its 39 icons (pan, guitar, drama, …) were removed after their 5 uses on the FPV page were switched to the new hobby icons.
- **The experiment is cleaned up.** Section "C · Choose · Hobby icons" (225 trial components), the loose pick copies and the noodles frame were removed from the FPV page.

## Why

There is now one clear icon per hobby, named after the hobby, in the main icon pack.
