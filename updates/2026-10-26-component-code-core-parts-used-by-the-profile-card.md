## Figma inconsistencies found (not changed)

- **Disabled states:** `sh-button` and `sh-icon-button` Disabled variants use flat greys instead of `sh-opacity/disabled`. The code follows the rule (FOUNDATION).
- **Translucent icon button:** `sh-icon-button` Translucent uses `icon/inverse` and `text/inverse` over photos, which turn dark in Dark mode. It should be `on-image`.
- **Stale descriptions:**
  - `sh-button` Small descriptions say 28 px tall; the variants are 32.
  - The `sh-tag` description lists old modes.
  - The `sh-tag-profile` blueprint calls You & Her and Astro "dark tag".
- **Dead properties:**
  - `sh-tag` "Show outline" isn't connected to any layer, and border weights are bound with no paint on White, Dark and Strong.
  - `sh-overlay` Photo full variants have none of their properties wired.
- **Wrong token for text:** the `sh-overlay` Photo requested status uses `indicator/success` as text colour (FOUNDATION §7.1 says indicators are never text).
- **Content overflow:** `sh-icon-button-with-label`'s root is fixed at 36 wide while its content is 40–44 wide.
- **Mixed strokes:** `sh-text-link` Neutral icon strokes are mixed, and Large uses the 12 icon scaled up.
- **Avatar placeholder Tint:** `sh-avatar-placeholder` Tint is bound straight to `sh-color-basic` (already known).
